Bilal Ahmad Naeem

Bilal Ahmad Naeem is a graduate student in the School of Architecture and Planning at the University of New Mexico, where he is pursuing a Master of Science in Architecture with a concentration in Digital Heritage and Historic Preservation. His research focuses on preserving and interpreting cultural heritage through advanced digital technologies, integrating architecture, archaeology, and reality-capture methods to create accurate digital records of historic places and artifacts.

His work centers on developing and refining digital documentation workflows using Mobile Laser Scanning (MLS), LiDAR, photogrammetry, Building Information Modeling (BIM), and Autodesk ReCap Pro. Through point cloud processing, 3D modeling, and data optimization, he supports the documentation, analysis, conservation, and interpretation of archaeological sites, historic buildings, and museum collections. His research aims to enhance how cultural heritage resources are recorded, studied, and shared with both professional and public audiences.

Before joining the University of New Mexico, Bilal worked as an architect, CAD instructor, and university lecturer in Pakistan. These experiences strengthened his expertise in architectural design, digital visualization, and education, while shaping his interdisciplinary approach to heritage documentation and preservation.

His research interests include digital heritage, reality capture, Mobile Laser Scanning, LiDAR, photogrammetry, BIM, 3D visualization, and immersive technologies for documenting and interpreting cultural landscapes and the built environment. His long-term goal is to advance digital documentation methodologies that make cultural heritage more accurately preserved, more accessible, and more meaningful for future generations.
