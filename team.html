Acknowledgments

The Cultural SITEs project was made possible through the collaboration of the University of New Mexico Historic Preservation & Regionalism Program, the New Mexico Humanities Council, and Northrop Grumman's Technology for Conservation initiative. Their support enabled interdisciplinary research, digital documentation, and public engagement efforts across culturally significant sites throughout New Mexico.

We extend our sincere gratitude to the archaeologists, historians, preservation professionals, tribal representatives, students, and community partners whose expertise and collaboration were essential throughout every phase of the project. Their knowledge and dedication helped ensure that each site was documented with accuracy, respect, and care.

Finally, we thank the many organizations and site stewards who welcomed our team and supported fieldwork, data collection, and educational outreach. Their commitment to preserving New Mexico's cultural heritage continues to inspire the work of the UNM Historic Preservation & Regionalism Digital Lab.
