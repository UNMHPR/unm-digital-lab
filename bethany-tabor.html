Bethany Tabor

Bethany Tabor is a multidisciplinary researcher, writer, and public engagement specialist based in New Mexico. Her work ranges from producing large-scale public events to facilitating panel conversations, project management, and writing for arts-based publications.

Her career has spanned ten years in the nonprofit arts and culture sector beginning in Brooklyn, New York, where she worked as Media Lab Manager and Head of Public Programs at Pioneer Works . While in New York, Tabor worked with artists across a range of practices helping them to develop projects, installations, and workshops geared toward community access and interaction.

She worked most closely with creative technologists and artists specializing in emergent media and interactive technology. In 2020, she relocated back to her hometown of Albuquerque, New Mexico to continue this work at the New Mexico Humanities Council as Senior Program Officer, where she managed public programs for their Digital Humanities Initiative. Her work at the Council was dedicated to bridging the gap between Humanities scholarship and innovations in digital technology and emergent media.

Tabor's research and writing focuses on place-based cultural practices; performance and time-based media; somatic inquiry; and considerations of the body in context. As a lifelong dancer turned movement researcher, Tabor's diverse career projects are all linked by her principal investigation of the body and its relationship to individual consciousness, life cycles, community, the natural and built environments, data, and technological progress.
